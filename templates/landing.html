What it does

01 · Face

Face & eye analysis

MediaPipe measures 468 landmarks in the browser and derives face shape, eye shape and the distances that decide which cuts suit you. The camera image never leaves your device.

02 · Wardrobe

Outfits that fit

Combines your measurements with the current temperature and weather, then suggests an outfit — collar, sleeve and length chosen for your proportions, not for a trend.

03 · Capsule

Climate capsule

Reads the climate normals for your coordinates and builds a twelve-month capsule: what the year actually demands where you live.

04 · Beauty

Makeup guidance

Contour, blush and lip placement derived from your bone structure, so the advice follows your face instead of a generic rule of thumb.

Where your data stays

Photos are processed locally Face measurement runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to analyse it.
Accounts stay on one server Your profile, wardrobe and settings live in a single database on the app's own host. No third-party profile exists.
No tracking, no ads No analytics scripts, no advertising identifiers, no cookies beyond your own session.
AI without training data Language and image requests are sent without your account data, and nothing is used for training.
