Privacy & your data

Aestra helps you choose outfits and beauty routines. Here is honestly where your data goes — nothing hidden.

Stays on your device

Face and landmark analysis runs in your browser (MediaPipe). Your camera frames are not uploaded for this. Your app settings and language stay local too.

Sent to our server and third parties

When you generate outfit or beauty suggestions, your text and — if you analyse a photo — that photo are sent to our server and forwarded to an AI provider (Google Gemini) for processing. Weather uses your approximate location via Open-Meteo. Shopping suggestions send your search term to a search service (Serper).

Stored on our server

Your account, wardrobe items (including uploaded photos), body profile and capsule are stored on our hosting provider, so they are available on any device you sign in from.

Your control

You can change your email and password, and delete your account and all of its data at any time (Settings → Delete account).

Goal: a more private architecture

This prototype already runs face analysis locally. A future version would also run the AI models locally, so that photos and text never leave your device.
